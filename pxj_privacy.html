Privacy Policy

Poker x Joker · VietGamePlay · Effective date: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Poker x Joker (package com.vietgameplay.pokerxjoker, the “Game”) and the third-party services it uses collect, how that information is used, and the choices you have. The Game is published by VietGamePlay (“we”, “us”).

  • The Game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive it.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects device and advertising information to serve and measure ads.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment card details.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress and settings locally on your device, for example levels unlocked, diamonds and gems, characters owned, whether you bought “remove ads”, and sound settings. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this game data in your device backup, which is stored by Google under your Google account and governed by Google’s privacy policy.

2. Information collected by third-party services

Advertising — Google AdMob

The Game shows banner and full-screen ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve, limit, and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as ads shown, taps, and diagnostics about ad performance.

Depending on your settings and the law in your region, ads may be personalised based on this information. Google’s use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you buy “remove ads”, the Game stops requesting ads.

In-app purchases — Google Play Billing

The Game offers optional in-app purchases (diamond packs and “remove ads”). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card or billing details. The Game receives only the information needed to deliver what you bought, such as the product purchased and a purchase confirmation from Google Play. Google’s handling of payment information is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not operate user accounts or a login.
  • We do not use our own analytics or tracking services.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. Device permissions

The Game requests only these Android permissions:

  • Internet and network state / Wi-Fi state — to load ads and to connect to Google Play for purchases.

5. How information is used

  • To save and restore your game progress on your device.
  • To deliver in-app purchases and restore “remove ads”.
  • To show ads that keep the Game free to play (through Google AdMob).

6. Your choices

  • Ad personalisation: on Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device’s settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). You can also manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • Remove ads: the optional “remove ads” purchase stops ads in the Game.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all game data stored on your device. Because we do not hold your game data on our servers, there is nothing further for us to delete; you may still contact us with any request.

7. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the Game. Information collected by Google AdMob and Google Play is retained by Google in line with Google’s own policies. Communication between the Game and these services uses their standard secure connections.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help address it.

9. International users

Google may process information collected by its services in countries other than your own. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under local law, such as the right to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights for information held by Google, use Google’s tools described above; for anything else, contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game’s store listing where appropriate.
