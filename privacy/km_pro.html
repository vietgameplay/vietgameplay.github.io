This Privacy Policy describes how VietGamePlay (“we”, “us”), the developer of the Android application Keyboard Mapper Pro (package com.vietgameplay.keyboardmapper.pro, the “App”), handles information in connection with the App. This policy applies only to this App. Our free app, KeyboardMapper (com.vgp.keyboardmapper), has its own privacy policy.

1. Summary

  • The App has no internet permission. It cannot send any data off your device, to us or to anyone else.
  • It contains no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases.
  • We run no servers and hold no user accounts. We collect no personal data.
  • Everything the App reads or creates stays on your device. The exceptions are a profile file you choose to share yourself, and Android's own backup if you have it turned on (sections 7 and 8).

2. Data stored on your device only

The App stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Key-mapping profiles and layouts: key codes, on-screen positions (stored as fractions of the screen size), gesture types, auto-click timing, and the package names of the games you assign to a profile.
  • Macros: the steps you create. Steps that type or search for text keep that text, and steps that open an app keep its name.
  • Image templates for “Find image” steps: small images you crop yourself from your screen, saved as PNG files.
  • App settings: for example the active profile, key size, theme, and your answer to the accessibility disclosure.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes all of this. Values a macro produces while it runs, such as text it found or copied, are kept in memory for that run only and are not saved.

3. Accessibility Service and what the App does with it

The App uses an Android Accessibility Service. It asks for your consent in the App before you enable it. It uses the service only for the features below, and only on your device.

CapabilityWhat it is used for
Filter key events Read the keys you press on a physical keyboard, so a key can act as a tap, hold or swipe, and for the App's keyboard shortcuts. Each key is checked in memory against your active profile; keys are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
Perform gestures Send the on-screen taps, holds and swipes that your key mappings and macros describe.
Window changes (app in the foreground) Notice which app came to the front, so the profile you linked to a game becomes active by itself. Only the package name is used, and it is not stored.
Retrieve window content Only while a macro you started runs a “Find text” step: search the text of the apps on screen for the words you entered, and type text into the field that has focus (“Input text”, “Paste text”, “Press Enter”).
Take screenshots Only while a macro runs a “Find image” step or a “Find text” step set to read the screen (OCR), when you crop an image template, or for a “Screenshot” step. For searches the screenshot is kept in memory, analysed on the device and discarded at once. A “Screenshot” step uses Android's own screenshot, which Android saves to your device like any screenshot you take yourself.

Text recognition (OCR) uses Google ML Kit's on-device text recognizer, which is bundled inside the App. It works offline, and because the App has no internet permission, nothing it reads leaves your device.

The App does not use the Accessibility Service to collect passwords, messages or other personal content, and does not use it for advertising, analytics or profiling. Macros only run when you start them. You can turn the service off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility.

4. Clipboard

A macro's “Copy text” step puts text on the Android clipboard, and its “Paste text” step pastes the clipboard into the field that has focus. The App touches the clipboard only for those steps.

5. Installed apps

To let you pick a game for a profile, open an app from a macro, or launch apps from the keyboard search box, the App lists the apps on your device that have a launcher icon. This list is built on the device when needed and is never stored or sent anywhere.

6. Permissions used

Permission / capabilityWhy it is used
Accessibility Service See section 3.
Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) Draw the on-screen key map while you play and while you edit a layout.
Package visibility for launcher apps (<queries>) List apps that have a launcher icon (section 5). The App does not request QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES.

The App does not request internet, network state, location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or the Advertising ID.

7. Sharing profiles

If you choose to share a profile, the App creates a .kmprofile.json file with that profile's keys, layouts and the package names of its games, and hands it to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. That app handles the file under its own privacy policy. Nothing is shared unless you do this.

8. Android backup

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the App's data (section 2) in your device backup, for example to your Google account. This is controlled by Android and your Google account settings, not by us, and we cannot access these backups. You can turn backup off in Android Settings.

9. Data we collect

We, VietGamePlay, collect no personal data. We do not sell or share any data, because we hold none.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Your rights
