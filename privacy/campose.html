Privacy Policy

CamPose: Pose Guide Camera · Last updated: November 28, 2026

This policy explains what information the CamPose app ("the app", "we") handles on iOS.

Summary. CamPose does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server.

1. Camera

The camera is used only to show the live preview and take photos inside the app. Camera frames are processed on your device and are never uploaded.

2. Photos

When you choose "From Library", the selected photo is analysed on your device to create a pose outline. The photo is not stored by CamPose or sent anywhere.

When you save a picture, CamPose only adds it to your photo library (add-only access). It cannot read your other photos.

3. Sharing

If you use Share, the picture is passed to the iOS share sheet and goes only to the app or person you choose.

4. Motion sensors

The Horizon Level guide reads your device's tilt to show a level line. This data stays on your device and is not recorded.

5. Children

CamPose does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
