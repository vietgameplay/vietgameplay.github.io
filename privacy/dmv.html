Privacy Policy

DMV Practice Test · Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information the DMV Practice Test app ("the app", "we") handles on iOS and Android. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Not a government app. DMV Practice Test is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any Department of Motor Vehicles or other government agency.

1. Information we do not collect

The app does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts.

2. Data stored on your device

Your selected vehicle type, state, settings, answers, test set scores and exam history are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Study plan and reminders. Your start date and test date are stored on your device. If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your device as local notifications; nothing is sent to us or to a push service. You can turn them off anytime in the app's Settings or in your device's notification settings.

Study Assistant. The Pro study assistant runs entirely on your device. The questions you type and its answers are processed on your phone and are not sent to us or to any AI service. The first time you use it, the app downloads the AI model file and the handbook for your selected state from our hosting provider; these downloads are ordinary file requests (your IP address is visible to the host, as with any web request) and contains no personal data.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment card details. The app only receives confirmation from the store of whether you have an active subscription.

4. Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (on iOS, only if you allow tracking);
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and operating system;
  • ad interactions such as impressions and taps;
  • crash and performance diagnostics of the ads SDK.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and certain US states), you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time. Subscribers to the Pro plan do not see ads.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used (for example, which screens are opened, app version, device model, OS version, country and session length) so we can fix problems and improve the app. Firebase assigns a random app-instance identifier; it does not receive your name or contact details. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6. Your choices

  • iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking lets you deny tracking. You can also reset your advertising identifier.
  • Android: Settings › Google › Ads lets you delete or reset your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads.
  • Subscribing to Pro removes all ads.

7. Children

The app is intended for people preparing for a driver's license, motorcycle or commercial driver's license knowledge test. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention and security

Because study data stays on your device, we do not retain it. Data processed by Google for advertising and analytics is handled under Google's retention policies; Firebase Analytics data is kept for the retention period configured in our Firebase project.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
