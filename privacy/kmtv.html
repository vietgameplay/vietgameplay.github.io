This Privacy Policy describes how VietGamePlay (“we”, “us”), the developer of the Android TV application Key Mapper (package com.vietgameplay.keyboardmapper.tv, the “App”), handles information in connection with the App. This policy applies only to this App.

1. Summary

The App does not collect, share or transmit any data. It has no Internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it has no user accounts. All of your configuration stays on your device.

2. Data stored on your device only

The App stores the following locally, in its private storage, and never sends it to us or anyone else:

  • Key-mapping profiles you create (key codes, on-screen positions stored as fractions of the screen size, gesture types and auto-click timing).
  • App settings: the active profile, key size and colour, theme, and whether you have accepted the Accessibility disclosure.

This data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. Accessibility Service and why the App needs it

The App uses an Android Accessibility Service for one purpose: to read key events from a physical keyboard connected to your TV and to dispatch the corresponding touch gestures on screen, so that a keyboard key can act as a tap, hold or swipe in a game. Specifically:

  • Key events are checked in memory against your active profile and are not recorded, stored, logged or transmitted. Keys from the TV remote (D-pad, OK, Back) are passed through unchanged.
  • The App does not read, collect or transmit screen content, text you type, passwords, or the content of other apps.
  • The App does not use the Accessibility Service for advertising, analytics or profiling.

The App asks for your consent before you turn the service on, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Accessibility on your TV.

4. Permissions used

Permission / capabilityWhy it is used
Accessibility Service (key event filtering, gesture dispatch) Turn physical key presses into on-screen touch input.
Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) Draw the on-screen key map while you play and while you edit a profile.

The App requests no other permissions. In particular it has no Internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permission, and it does not use the Advertising ID.

5. Purchase

The App is a paid app sold through Google Play. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy; we never receive or store your payment details. The App itself contains no billing or licensing code and does not contact any server.

6. Data we collect and share

None. We collect no personal or device data, and we do not sell or share any data, because we receive none.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is no account or record for us to export or delete. Data stored locally by the App can be deleted at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. For any question about your privacy, contact us at vietgameplay@gmail.com.
