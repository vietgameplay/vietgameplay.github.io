This Privacy Policy describes how VietGamePlay (“we”, “us”), the developer of the Android application Auto Clicker: Macros (package com.vietgameplay.autoclicker, the “App”), handles information in connection with the App. This policy applies only to this App.

1. Summary

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data on our own servers. We operate no backend service and hold no user accounts. Your clickers, macros and settings stay on your device. The App includes third-party services from Google (advertising and in-app purchases) which process data under their own privacy policies, as described in section 5.

2. Data stored on your device only

The App stores the following locally, in its private storage, and never sends it to us:

  • Clickers and macros you create: their steps, tap and swipe positions (stored as fractions of the screen size), timing, repeat and stop-timer settings, any text a step types or searches for, and small images you crop from your own screen for “Find image” steps.
  • App settings, such as which clicker the floating panel plays, whether action indicators are shown, and a cached flag indicating whether you purchased the Pro upgrade.

This data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. Accessibility Service and why the App needs it

The App uses an Android Accessibility Service (the core function of an auto clicker) for exactly these purposes, and only after you have read an in-app disclosure and turned the service on yourself in Android Settings:

  • Performing touches for you: tapping, holding and swiping at the spots you placed, only while a clicker or macro you started is running.
  • Showing the floating panel and the on-screen target markers over other apps (as accessibility overlays, so no “display over other apps” permission is needed).
  • Running the macros you start: only while a macro you started is running, the App may read the text shown on screen (to find what a “Find text” step looks for), take a screenshot (for “Find image” and on-screen text recognition steps, examined on your device with Google ML Kit's bundled, offline recognizer), type or paste text into the field you are in, use the clipboard for “Copy text” / “Paste text” steps, open an app you chose for an “Open app” step, and perform Back / Home actions.

Screen content and screenshots are processed in memory on your device and are never stored or transmitted. The App does not read what you type or touch, does not collect passwords or the content of other apps, and does not use the Accessibility Service for advertising, analytics or profiling.

The “Anti-detection” option only randomises the position and timing of the App's own taps; it does not read or alter any other app's data.

You can turn the Accessibility Service off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility. Without it, clickers and macros cannot run.

5. Third-party services

The free version of the App displays advertising, and the App offers an optional one-time in-app purchase (Pro). These are provided by Google and process data independently of us:

  • Google AdMob (ads, free version only) may collect and process device identifiers including the Advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure ads. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Ads are not shown to users who purchase Pro.
  • Google Play Billing processes your one-time purchase of Pro. We never receive or store your payment card or billing details; the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google ML Kit text recognition runs entirely on your device with a bundled model; the App does not send screenshots or recognised text to Google or anyone else.

You can reset or limit your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

6. Data we collect

We, VietGamePlay, collect no personal data ourselves. We operate no analytics, crash reporting or tracking of our own, and we do not sell or share any data, because we hold none.

7. Children

For questions about data processed by Google as described in section 5, please refer to Google's privacy policy and account controls. For any privacy request, contact us at vietgameplay@gmail.com.
