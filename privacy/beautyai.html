Privacy Policy – BeautyAI

App: BeautyAI (Android package and iOS bundle ID com.vgp.beautyai)
Developer: VietGamePlay ("we", "us")
Effective date: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the BeautyAI app ("the App") uses, why, and the choices you have. The App is a live makeup and face beauty camera: it shows virtual makeup and beauty effects on your face in real time and lets you save or share photos.

Summary. Camera images and face data are processed only on your device, in real time, and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We do not operate our own servers and do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location. Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier.

1. Camera and face data

The App needs access to your device's front camera to show the live makeup preview.

  • On-device processing only. Each camera frame is analysed on your device to find the position of your face, eyes, eyebrows, lips, skin and hair, so effects can be drawn in the right place. On Android this uses Google's on-device MediaPipe Face Landmarker; on iPhone and iPad it uses Apple's ARKit face tracking (TrueDepth camera, where available) and the Vision framework.
  • Not stored, not shared. Face geometry, facial landmarks and camera frames are used only for the current frame and are discarded immediately. They are not saved, not sent off your device, not used to identify you, and not used for advertising, analytics or training any model.
  • We do not use face data for facial recognition or to create any biometric profile.

2. Photos

When you tap the capture button, the App creates a photo with the effects applied. The photo is saved to your device's photo gallery only when you choose to save it, and is shared only when you choose to share it using your device's share sheet (in which case it is sent to the app you pick). We do not receive copies of your photos. On iOS the App asks for photo library permission to save photos; on Android 9 and earlier it asks for storage permission for the same purpose.

3. Information stored on your device

The App saves a small amount of information on your device so it works between sessions:

  • Whether you have completed the introduction screens, how many times the App has been opened, when the subscription screen was last shown, and how many free tries you have used.
  • Whether you have an active Premium subscription or have purchased "Remove Ads", so the App can unlock features.

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes it. Your makeup choices during a session are not stored.

4. Subscriptions and in-app purchases

The App offers optional Premium subscriptions (monthly and 6-month, which may include a free trial for new subscribers) and a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase, through Google Play Billing on Android or the Apple App Store on iOS. Payments are handled entirely by Google or Apple; we never receive your payment card or bank details. The App receives only the information needed to unlock what you bought (such as the product ID and purchase status), and checks it on your device.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can cancel at any time in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings (Settings > your name > Subscriptions).

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner and interstitial ads provided by Google AdMob, unless you purchase "Remove Ads". To deliver, measure and (where permitted) personalise ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID). On iOS the App does not ask for permission to track you, so your IDFA is not made available to ads;
  • Device and app information (for example device model, operating system, app version) and diagnostic information;
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Interactions with ads.

Camera images, face data and photos are never shared with AdMob. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

7. What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers or databases for the App, and we do not receive your camera images, face data or photos.
  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it where we can.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • Camera and face data: not retained; discarded after each frame.
  • Photos: kept only in your gallery, if you saved them; you can delete them at any time.
  • On your device: settings and purchase status are kept until you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google according to Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID at any time.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or other local data protection laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data on our own servers, most of these rights are exercised directly with Google or Apple (see sections 4–5) or by deleting the App's data on your device. For any question or request, contact us using the details below.

11. Security

Face processing happens entirely on your device, so camera and face data never travel over the network. Data sent to Google or Apple services is protected in transit by those services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the information the App stores to the minimum it needs.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the App's store listing or in the App.
