Blox Strike

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026 · Applies to the Blox Strike app for iOS and Android

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Blox Strike mobile game ("Blox Strike", "the app") stores and processes, why, and the choices you have. Blox Strike is published by VietGamePlay ("we", "us").

In short: Blox Strike has no user accounts and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress stays on your device. To show ads, measure how the game is used, process purchases and run the leaderboard, the app uses services from Google and Apple, which may collect device information as described below. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

VietGamePlay
Da Nang, Viet Nam
Email: vietgameplay@gmail.com

2. Information stored only on your device

The following is saved in the app's local storage on your phone or tablet. It is not sent to us.

  • Your character's look, chosen character model and weapon loadout, and your selected map.
  • Your coin balance, unlocked maps and whether you have bought "Remove Ads".
  • Settings: language, music, sound effects and parachute-drop options.
  • Your best runs on this device (kills, wave, map and date), shown in the "This device" ranking.

This information is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. Consent for ads and tracking

  • Europe (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland: before ads load, the app shows Google's consent form, where you can accept or refuse personalized ads. You can change your choice later through the same form where it is offered.
  • iOS: the app asks for permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you decline, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared and ads are not personalized based on it. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Buying Remove Ads turns off all ads in the game.

5. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account.
  • We do not access your precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

6. How information is used

  • To run the game and keep your progress on your device.
  • To deliver in-app purchases and restore Remove Ads.
  • To show ads, which keep the game free, and to limit how often full-screen ads appear.
  • To measure game usage and stability and decide what to improve.
  • To show your score on the global leaderboard when you are signed in to Game Center or Play Games.

7. Data retention

Information on your device stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Information collected by Google and Apple is kept according to their own retention policies and, for Firebase Analytics, according to the retention settings of our analytics project.

8. International transfers

The third-party services above may process information on servers outside Viet Nam, including in the United States, under their own safeguards.

9. Children

Blox Strike is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or restrict its processing. Because we do not hold accounts or game data on our own servers, most information can be removed by uninstalling the app and resetting your advertising ID. For analytics data or any other request, email us at vietgameplay@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

11. Security

We rely on the security measures of the platforms and services listed above. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we do not transmit your game progress to any server of our own.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or the services it uses change. The new version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
